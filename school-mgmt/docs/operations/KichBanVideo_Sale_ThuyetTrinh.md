# Kich Ban Video Sale

## Muc tieu

Tai lieu nay la kich ban thuyet trinh cho video sale theo format da chot:

1. mo dau bang muc tieu video;
2. ve roadmap tong the cua role sale;
3. di lan luot tung chang nghiep vu co scene briefing;
4. mo man hinh that, thao tac that va long tieng tieng Viet theo tung buoc;
5. ket lai bang phan tong hop de dung cho onboarding va demo he thong.

## Cau truc video

### Canh 1. Mo dau

Man hinh:

- title card mo dau.

Loi dan:

> Xin chao. Day la video sale duoc san xuat theo quy trinh moi. Video se bat dau bang so do luong tong the, sau do di lan luot qua tung nhom cong viec chinh cua sale voi chu thich tieng Viet va thao tac that tren he thong.

### Canh 2. So do luong tong the

Man hinh:

- roadmap card gom cac chang:
  `Dashboard -> Sale Hub -> Leads -> Conversations -> Order khach moi -> Order khach cu -> Duyet don -> Invoice va Student -> Sessions -> Trial offline -> Commission -> Dang xuat`

Loi dan:

> Luong chuan cua sale bat dau tu dashboard va sale hub, sau do chuyen sang leads va chatbot, tiep tuc len order cho khach moi hoac khach cu, gui duyet, kiem tra invoice va student phat sinh, xu ly phat sinh lien quan toi session, tao trial offline, doi chieu commission va cuoi cung la dang xuat.

## Phan thao tac chi tiet

### Chang 1. Doc dashboard dau ngay

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- dashboard sale.

Loi dan scene:

> Chang dau tien la khoi dong ngay lam viec. Sale vao dashboard de biet hom nay can uu tien lead nao, follow-up nao va tinh hinh doanh so, hoa hong hien tai ra sao.

Loi dan thao tac:

> O dashboard sale, nguoi dung doc ty le chuyen doi, doanh thu, hoa hong, follow-up hom nay va cac don gan day. Day la man hinh de chot uu tien lam viec truoc khi mo tung module rieng.

### Chang 2. Xem Sale Hub va quy trinh chuan

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- sale hub.

Loi dan scene:

> Sau dashboard, sale can nhin lai sale hub de luon nho dung thu tu thao tac. Day la diem dac biet quan trong voi sale moi, vi he thong co nhieu man hinh nhung phai di dung luong.

Loi dan thao tac:

> Sale hub la man hinh tom tat quy trinh chuan. Nguoi xem chi can nhin vao hero card, nam workflow card va gate tai chinh la hieu ngay vi sao phai di theo thu tu parent, student, order, duyet hoac nap tien roi moi sang lop.

### Chang 3. Lam viec voi leads

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- module leads;
- lead detail.

Loi dan scene:

> Khi da nam dung luong, sale quay ve CRM de lam viec voi lead. Chang nay tap trung vao viec tim dung lead can xu ly va doc du boi canh truoc khi tu van.

Loi dan thao tac:

> O module leads, sale dung bo loc va keyword de tim dung lead. Sau do mo detail de xem phu huynh, hoc sinh, source, estimated value va lich su lien he truoc khi tiep tuc cham soc hoac chuyen doi.

### Chang 4. Tiep quan hoi thoai chatbot

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- conversations;
- sidebar tao lead.

Loi dan scene:

> Khong phai lead nao cung vao truc tiep tu form. Mot phan lon den tu chatbot va fanpage. Vi vay sale can biet cach tiep quan hoi thoai va chuyen no thanh lead chinh thuc.

Loi dan thao tac:

> Trong conversations, sale chon mot hoi thoai AI dang xu ly, bam tiep quan roi dung form tao lead o sidebar. Cach lam nay giup noi lien kenh marketing va CRM ngay trong cung mot flow.

### Chang 5. Tao order cho khach moi

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- orders;
- modal tao order.

Loi dan scene:

> Sau khi lead du dieu kien, sale bat dau len order. Tinh huong dau tien la khach hoan toan moi, nghia la parent va student deu chua co san trong he thong.

Loi dan thao tac:

> O tinh huong khach moi, sale tao order tu dau voi phu huynh moi, hoc sinh moi, goi hoc phu hop va chung tu thanh toan. Sau khi luu nhap, don se xuat hien tren bang de sale ra lai truoc khi submit.

### Chang 6. Tao order cho khach cu

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- orders;
- modal tao order cho khach cu.

Loi dan scene:

> Tinh huong thu hai la khach cu. Day la nhanh sale dung rat thuong xuyen, vi he thong cho phep tai dung parent va student co san de tranh tao trung ho so.

Loi dan thao tac:

> Voi khach cu, sale dung chuc nang lookup de tim parent va student da co. Sau do van thao tac chon product, lop va chung tu nhu mot order binh thuong, nhung toan bo du lieu goc duoc tai su dung.

### Chang 7. Duyet order

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- doi vai sang director;
- modal duyet.

Loi dan scene:

> Khi sale gui duyet, quyen xu ly duoc chuyen sang Director hoac OPS. Day la khau kiem tra chung tu va xac nhan order co the di xuong downstream hay chua.

Loi dan thao tac:

> O goc nhin Director, order duoc mo lai tu danh sach cho duyet. Nguoi duyet ra chung tu, xac nhan va he thong se ghi nhan trang thai moi cung cac du lieu phat sinh lien quan.

### Chang 8. Kiem tra invoice va student phat sinh

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- invoices;
- students.

Loi dan scene:

> Khi order da duoc duyet, sale khong dung lai o do. Chang tiep theo la quay lai kiem tra cac du lieu phat sinh nhu invoice va student de chac chan luong downstream da chay dung.

Loi dan thao tac:

> Dau tien, sale mo module invoices de tim lai hoa don vua phat sinh tu order da duoc duyet. Sau do sale mo module students de xac nhan ho so hoc sinh moi da duoc provision dung.

### Chang 9. Gui yeu cau thay doi session

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- sessions;
- session detail;
- change request form.

Loi dan scene:

> Ngoai lead va order, sale con tham gia vao cac phat sinh lien quan toi buoi hoc. Tinh huong dien hinh la gui yeu cau thay doi giao vien hoac thay doi buoi cho mot session dang duoc len lich.

Loi dan thao tac:

> O module sessions, sale mo dung buoi hoc dang scheduled, vao khu vuc change request, chon giao vien de xuat va ghi ro ly do thay doi. Day la cach chuan de chuyen phat sinh cho nguoi duyet tiep nhan.

### Chang 10. Tao trial enrollment offline

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- trial enrollments;
- modal tao hoc thu.

Loi dan scene:

> Mot nghiep vu khac cua sale la tao ban ghi hoc thu offline. Day la nhanh dung truoc khi khoa hoc chinh thuc duoc chot thanh order hoac invoice hoan chinh.

Loi dan thao tac:

> O module trial enrollments, sale tao mot ban ghi hoc thu offline voi day du hoc vien, phu huynh, lop va goi san pham. Ban ghi nay giup luong hoc thu duoc theo doi tach biet truoc khi chot dang ky chinh thuc.

### Chang 11. Doi chieu commission report

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- commission report.

Loi dan scene:

> Chang gan cuoi la doi chieu hieu qua ban hang. Sale vao commission report de doc doanh thu, hoa hong, phan cho duyet va tung dong don hang lien quan.

Loi dan thao tac:

> O bao cao hoa hong, sale xem tong doanh thu, tong hoa hong, phan dang cho duyet va tung don chi tiet. Day la noi noi ket cong viec chot don hang ngay voi ket qua tai chinh cuoi ky.

### Chang 12. Dang xuat

Man hinh:

- scene card;
- shell ung dung;
- login page.

Loi dan scene:

> Chang cuoi cung la dang xuat de dong phien lam viec. Khi he thong quay ve man hinh login, chuoi thao tac cua role sale duoc xem la hoan tat.

Loi dan thao tac:

> Cuoi cung, sale dang xuat khoi he thong de ket thuc phien lam viec. Day la diem dung dung sau khi da di qua dashboard, leads, orders, downstream va cac nghiep vu bo tro.

## Canh ket

Man hinh:

- title card ket thuc.

Loi dan:

> Nhu vay chung ta vua di qua toan bo luong cong viec chinh cua sale, tu nhin dashboard va sale hub, xu ly lead va chatbot, len order, gui duyet, kiem tra du lieu phat sinh, cho toi cac nghiep vu trial, sessions va commission.
