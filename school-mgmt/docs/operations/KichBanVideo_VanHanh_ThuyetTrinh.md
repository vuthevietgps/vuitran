# Kich Ban Video Van Hanh Thuyet Trinh

## Muc tieu video

Video nay mo ta toan bo luong cong viec chinh cua nhan vien van hanh theo format:

`intro -> roadmap -> scene briefing -> thao tac that tren UI -> outro`

Muc tieu la giup nguoi xem:

- hieu nghiep vu truoc khi thay thao tac;
- biet OPS phai vao man hinh nao truoc, man hinh nao sau;
- thay ro moi diem thao tac chinh bang caption, callout va long tieng.

## Roadmap tong the

1. Dashboard
2. Classes
3. Sessions bulk create
4. Sessions finalize
5. Attendance
6. Ticket intake
7. Teacher swap
8. Ticket resolve
9. Logout

## Intro

Loi thuyet minh:

Video nay huong dan toan bo luong cong viec chinh cua role van hanh. Chung ta se di tu dashboard dau ngay, sang classes, sessions, attendance, ticket, dieu phoi giao vien thay the, dong ticket va ket thuc bang dang xuat.

## Scene 1. Dashboard dau ngay

Muc tieu:

- xac dinh viec uu tien trong ngay;
- doc canh bao qua han;
- nhin nhanh so lop dang van hanh va cac muc cho xu ly.

Loi thuyet minh:

OPS bat dau tu dashboard de biet ngay viec nao can xu ly som. Day la man hinh dinh huong dau ca, giup uu tien dung ticket, dung lop va dung request cho phep.

## Scene 2. Kiem tra lop tren Classes

Muc tieu:

- mo dung lop phat sinh;
- kiem tra roster;
- them hoc vien va doc lai du lieu ngay tren bang lop.

Loi thuyet minh:

Sau dashboard, OPS quay ve nguon du lieu lop hoc. Tat ca thao tac roster va giao vien phu trach phai duoc cap nhat ngay tai day de sessions va attendance dung chung mot du lieu chuan.

## Scene 3. Tao lich hoc hang loat tren Sessions

Muc tieu:

- chon lop;
- chon ngay va khung gio;
- sinh them session moi cho tuan tiep theo.

Loi thuyet minh:

Khi lop da dung du lieu, OPS tao lich hoc hang loat de bao dam lop co du buoi sap toi. Sau khi luu, danh sach session phai tang them de xac nhan lich moi da duoc ghi nhan.

## Scene 4. Chot buoi hoc da hoan thanh

Muc tieu:

- loc dung buoi dang cho chot;
- mo detail;
- finalize buoi hoc.

Loi thuyet minh:

Voi cac buoi da co attendance va teaching report, OPS la nguoi chot buoi de khoa trang thai van hanh. Buoc nay anh huong truc tiep den payroll va doi soat nen phai thao tac dung thu tu.

## Scene 5. Diem danh tren Attendance

Muc tieu:

- chon dung lop va dung ngay;
- tai danh sach hoc vien;
- luu attendance va doc lai summary.

Loi thuyet minh:

Attendance la noi OPS xac nhan du lieu co mat, vang mat hay di muon cua hoc vien. Sau khi luu, summary phai phan anh dung tong so hoc vien da duoc ghi nhan.

## Scene 6. Tiep nhan ticket

Muc tieu:

- mo dung ticket phat sinh;
- doc boi canh goc;
- de lai comment dieu phoi.

Loi thuyet minh:

Khi co phat sinh tu giao vien hoac phu huynh, ticket la noi OPS tiep nhan va luu dau vet xu ly. Comment dieu phoi la diem xac nhan da nhan viec va dang xu ly gi.

## Scene 7. Dieu phoi giao vien thay the

Muc tieu:

- quay lai lop lien quan;
- chon giao vien thay the;
- luu va kiem tra bang lop da doi nguoi day.

Loi thuyet minh:

Sau khi tiep nhan ticket, OPS phai xu ly ngay tai nguon. Voi tinh huong giao vien nghi dot xuat, nguon du lieu can cap nhat la lop hoc, khong chi la ticket.

## Scene 8. Dong ticket

Muc tieu:

- mo lai ticket da xu ly;
- nhap tom tat ket qua;
- chuyen ticket sang resolved.

Loi thuyet minh:

Khi dieu phoi da xong, OPS quay lai ticket de khep vong xu ly. Ticket chi nen dong sau khi da co ket qua thuc te va co tom tat ro rang cho nguoi doc sau.

## Scene 9. Dang xuat

Muc tieu:

- ket thuc phien thao tac;
- dua he thong ve man hinh login.

Loi thuyet minh:

Khi he thong quay ve man hinh dang nhap, chuoi thao tac chinh cua role van hanh duoc xem la hoan tat.

## Outro

Loi thuyet minh:

Nhu vay chung ta vua di qua toan bo luong cong viec chinh cua OPS, tu dashboard, lop hoc, sessions, attendance cho toi ticket va dieu phoi giao vien. Video nay co the dung de dao tao, onboarding hoac nghiem thu giao dien role van hanh.
